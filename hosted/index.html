CONTEXT YOUR AGENTS CAN USE.04 / KNOWLEDGE

A reference shelf
for your agents.

Project notes, policies, product briefs or study material. Add once. Retrieve the relevant passages when a task needs them.

Up to 30,000 characters per document, 12 documents. PDFs up to 15 MB / 60 pages; selectable text only, no OCR. Semantic search is not supported yet. Documents stay in your signed-in workspace until removed. Existing runs keep retrieved excerpts for up to one hour.

PDF text is extracted in your browser; the original file is not uploaded. Review the text before saving. Adding stores the text on AgentOS hosting. Selected matching excerpts are sent to Gemini when you run a task. Only add material you intend to use with this service.
